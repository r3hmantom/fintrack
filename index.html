Privacy

Your money stays on your phone

Raqza is local-first. There's no account to create and no server holding your finances.

  • No sign-up, no email. Open it and start tracking.
  • Works fully offline. The internet is only used for exchange rates, and only currency codes and dates are sent.
  • Anonymous usage is off by default. If you turn it on, it never includes amounts, names, notes or categories.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to create an account?

No. Raqza works without sign-up or email. Your records live in a database on your phone.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, everything works offline. When you're online, Raqza fetches daily exchange rates for accounts in other currencies. Only currency codes and dates are sent, never your amounts.

Do my friends need the app to split bills?

No. Add people by name and Raqza keeps track of who paid and who owes whom. You record repayments when they happen.

Can I move my data to a new phone?

Yes. Settings › Backup & restore exports everything as one JSON file. Importing it on another phone adds your data and never overwrites what's already there.

Which currencies are supported?

Pakistani rupee by default, plus US dollars, euros, pounds, dirhams, riyals and many more. Each account has its own currency.

Is there an iPhone version?

Raqza is Android-first. An iPhone version is planned.
